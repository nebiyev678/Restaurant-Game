Privacy Policy

Restaurant Empire · Last updated 23 November 2026

In short: Restaurant Empire does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. Your game progress stays on your own device.

This policy explains how the Restaurant Empire game (the Android app and the web version at nebiyev678.github.io/Restaurant-Game) handles information. It is published by the developer of Restaurant Empire (GitHub user nebiyev678).

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Game data stored on your device

To save your progress, the game stores the following on your device only (in the app's local storage):

  • your career progress (cash, level, unlocked recipes, staff, purchases, missions and challenges);
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics quality);
  • the time you last played, used to calculate offline franchise earnings.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, clearing the app's storage, or clearing your browser's site data deletes it permanently.

Internet connections

The game works offline. When you are online, it downloads its text fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google receives your device's IP address and basic browser information when the fonts are fetched. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ. The game makes no other network requests.

If you download the game from Google Play, Google handles the download and updates under its own policies.

Device permissions

  • Vibration: short vibrations as gameplay feedback. You can turn this off in the game's Settings or Pause menu.
  • Internet: only for the font download described above.

The game does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Sharing and selling

We do not share or sell any information, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, adding online features), this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the game's GitHub page: github.com/nebiyev678/Restaurant-Game/issues.

Restaurant Empire is an independent game and is not affiliated with Google.
